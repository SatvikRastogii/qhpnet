# Related-work notes (verified 2026-10-25)

Source of details: abstracts via Crossref/OpenAlex/Europe PMC; setup numbers from open-access full text (PMC) where marked "full text". "Abstract only" = publisher page was blocked (403/redirect), so setup was NOT verified and is left blank.

## Recent papers (2022-2026)

1. **Jing 2024, HHOCFR** (IEEE Access 12:12149-12163, doi 10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3354276). Improved Harris hawks optimisation selects CHs and forms clusters jointly (no extra messages); fuzzy logic chooses relay CH; CH rotation vs re-clustering by energy monitoring. Gain: lifetime +34.20/28.11/23.65/17.43% over IBRE-LEACH, DAPFL, IHHO-F, HHO-UCRA; throughput +10.9 to 21%. Abstract only: nodes/area/BS not verified. Limitation: no channel-loss or statistical-test claim in abstract.
2. **Rami Reddy 2023, EECHIGWO** (Computers 12(2):35, doi 10.3390/computers12020035). Improved GWO for CH selection using sink distance, residual energy, CH balance, intra-cluster distance; reports dead nodes, rounds, throughput. Abstract only (setup, baselines not verified). Limitation: single-hop-style CH selection, no routing layer in abstract.
3. **Singh & Gupta 2022, WTSA** (New Rev. Inf. Netw. 27(1):1-29, doi 10.1080/13614576.2022.2039748). Whale + tunicate swarm hybrid for multi-objective CH selection and multipath routing (delay, inter/intra-cluster distance, degree, energy). Reported delay 0.140 s and throughput 69,641 kbps at 100 nodes. Abstract only. Limitation: no FND/HND/LND in abstract, baselines not verified.
4. **Sennan et al. 2025, MOGWO** (Sci. Rep. 16:179, doi 10.1038/s41598-025-28950-2; full text). Multi-objective GWO with fuzzy fitness (distance, neighbours, residual energy) for CH selection in IoT-WSN. Setup: 500x500 m, 300 nodes, MATLAB 2019a, 30 runs, BS at (250,0) and (250,250); baselines LEACH, IFFOA, SHO-CH, SHO-OBL. Gain: lifetime +10-20%, PDR +2-10%, overhead -5-10%; FND/HND/LND tabulated (e.g. FND 2500 vs 2200 for best baseline in one scenario). Limitation: single-hop fading model mention only, no significance test.
5. **Nareshkumar et al. 2026, RL-MHWO-GWO** (Sci. Rep. 16:28157, doi 10.1038/s41598-026-58774-7; full text). DRL agent for sleep scheduling/node activation; WOA-GWO hybrid for CH selection; multipath routing CH-to-BS. Setup: 100-250 nodes, BS at centre, baselines LEACH, WAOA, AHCS-GWO, IQ-ABC, EMO-PEGASIS; FND and LND reported; path loss metric (73 dB). Limitation (authors): imprecise measurement of residual energy, signal power and proximity; no test of significance seen.
6. **Wang et al. 2025, improved SSA** (Sci. Rep. 15:12517, doi 10.1038/s41598-025-96613-3; full text). Sparrow search improved with adaptive sine and Cauchy terms for CH selection (5-term fitness) plus multi-hop forwarding. Setup: 200x200, 200 nodes, 0.5 J, BS outside field; baselines LEACH, LEACH-C, LEACH-SSA, LEACH-SSSA. FND 1050 (last row of table, presumed proposed) vs 289 for LEACH. Limitation: HND/LND defined non-standardly (40% / 80% dead), so figures are not comparable to the usual definition.
7. **Abdulkareem et al. 2025, BSBOC** (Sensors 25(14):4339, doi 10.3390/s25144339; full text). Binary secretary bird optimisation CH selection with a Voronoi-diagram fitness term. Setup: 100x100 m, 100 nodes (10% advanced in heterogeneous case), sink at centre or (100,100); baselines MOBGWO, WAOA. FND 866 vs 456/606 (1.9x / 1.43x) in homogeneous scenario 1. Wilcoxon test used on FND/HND. Limitation: only two baselines, small field, clustering only.
8. **Mohammed et al. 2026a, BA-ABC** (Sensors 26(8):2401, doi 10.3390/s26082401; full text). Bat (exploitation) + ABC (exploration) hybrid for joint clustering and routing. Setup: 100 nodes, 1000x1000 m, 2 J, first-order radio model, 30 runs, MATLAB R2024a. Gain: energy -22-30%, lifetime +18-25%, latency about -24% vs ACO and others; confidence intervals and hypothesis testing reported. Limitation (authors): simulation only, no testbed, parameter tuning is manual.
9. **Mohammed et al. 2026b, MARL-BA-ABC** (Sensors 26(15):4996, doi 10.3390/s26154996; full text). Adds multi-agent RL for adaptive routing on top of BA-ABC CH selection; objective uses link quality, load, density. Setup: 100-1000 nodes, 1000x1000 m, 30 seeds; baselines LEACH, HEED, PSO, ACO, BA, ABC. FND 2200 rounds vs LEACH 1200 (+83.3%), HEED 1450, PSO 1700, ACO 1800. ANOVA/Wilcoxon/effect size designed but the paper itself says it reports only descriptive statistics. Limitation: inferential results not shown; loss model not verified.
10. **Belloula et al. 2026, SMA-WSN** (Sensors 26(18):5776, doi 10.3390/s26185776; full text). Slime Mould Algorithm (not Physarum transport) selects CHs from a tri-objective fitness (residual energy, BS proximity, coverage); failure-aware. Setup: NS-3.47, N=30 nodes, six agricultural topologies, five failure rates, 50 seeds, 18,000 runs, 11 baselines, BS at field border. Gain: highest per-hop PDR at every failure rate, but ranks 7th of 12 on end-to-end delivery, tied with leaders on Jain fairness (0.922). Limitation (authors): propagation is deterministic (no shadowing/fading), centralised election, small N, weight tuning overlaps evaluation terrain.
11. **Mathankumar et al. 2026, AVAQOA** (Sci. Rep. 16:22529, doi 10.1038/s41598-026-62382-w; full text). African vulture optimiser for clustering, Aquila optimiser for CH-to-sink route. Setup: 50-500 nodes, BS at (500,500), 1500x1500 m (also 1000x1000); baselines FLION, HM-ACOPSO, TABU-PSO, PPSOA. FND +9.32-14.28%, LND +10.32-14.58%; FND/HND/LND reported. Limitation: no significance testing seen; ideal link assumption not discussed.
12. **Liu 2026, QPSODRL** (Sci. Rep. 16:5526, doi 10.1038/s41598-026-35365-0). Entropy-guided quantum PSO for clustering plus a dueling double DQN (D3QN) for routing. Abstract only (setup, gains not verified). Relevant as an RL+metaheuristic baseline.
13. **Kaushal & Malik 2025, HOAM** (Int. J. Commun. Syst. 38(16), doi 10.1002/dac.70268). Hippopotamus optimisation selects CHs and optimises mobile-sink position in a WSN. Abstract only: setup/gains not verified. This is the only WSN clustering paper found that uses the HO algorithm for CH selection.

## Closest prior work (novelty check)

Searches (Crossref, OpenAlex, Europe PMC, web; terms: quorum sensing + WSN/clustering/routing, Physarum + WSN, hippopotamus optimisation + WSN, refractory/fatigue + cluster head) found **no WSN paper combining bacterial quorum sensing, a metaheuristic and Physarum routing**. Caveat: search is not exhaustive (Scopus/Web of Science/IEEE Xplore were not queryable).

- **Quorum sensing in WSN: nothing relevant.** "Quorum" hits (e.g. Ponnan et al., IEEE Sensors J. 21(15):17373, doi 10.1109/JSEN.2021.3080217; Energy-Balance Quorum System 2017) are *quorum systems* for duty-cycle/grid scheduling, not bacterial quorum sensing. Only a weak lead: Balasubramaniam et al., "Chemotaxis and Quorum Sensing Inspired Device Interaction Supporting Social Networking", VTC-Spring 2007 (doi 10.1109/VETECS.2007.66), about mobile-device interaction, not WSN clustering; its content was not read, so it is not in refs.bib.
- **Physarum in WSN routing (no CH selection, no metaheuristic, no QS):** Zhang et al. 2013 P-iRP (doi 10.1155/2013/483581); Li et al. 2011 slime-mould routing (doi 10.1007/s11721-011-0063-y). Both are in refs.bib.
- **Slime-mould-inspired clustering:** Belloula 2026 SMA-WSN (item 10). Uses the Slime Mould Algorithm as a CH optimiser; no Physarum flow adaptation, no quorum mechanism.
- **Hippopotamus optimisation for WSN CHs:** Kaushal & Malik 2025 (item 13); no bio-inspired routing adaptation or QS.
- **Metaheuristic + learning, joint clustering/routing:** Mohammed 2026b (item 9), Nareshkumar 2026 (item 5), Liu 2026 (item 12).

**Verdict:** closest prior work is the pairing of (a) Kaushal & Malik 2025 (HO for WSN CH selection) with (b) Zhang et al. 2013 / Li et al. 2011 (Physarum/slime-mould routing). Each covers one ingredient; neither adds quorum-sensing-based density/threshold coordination, nor combines them. A claim of novelty should be worded as "to the best of our knowledge, and based on searches of Crossref/OpenAlex/Europe PMC".
